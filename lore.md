The ancient people, whom we call the Ummum, tell stories of the Angels. It is said that they lived in great palaces amongst the stars. Many aeons ago there was a war in the heavens, and now few Angels remain. There are those who believe that the people living here now are descendents of these Angels, though who can say for sure?<br>

Life in the jungle is often hard; there is a reason why few venture outside of The Ring. Though it is true enough that the caravans travel between the city states, most do well to stay by the great lakes that provide us sustenance. For the jungle has only continued to impose on us, and it is all we can do to fight back the encroaching growth year by year.<br>

At one point in the distant past, the Ummum were free to spread across the world, far beyond the great lakes. Now what remains of their cities can only be found by those daring enough to leave the safety of the lakes, and venture into the jungle.<br>
