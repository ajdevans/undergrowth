---
title: Venturing South
permalink: /2026-11-02/
---

With Sir Reginald Faetoucher and Julius Pines predisposed for the week, the party consisted of **Jonathan Don-Geon** (Hermit, Lvl 1), **Balthazar S. Hammer** (Miner, Lvl 1), and a new addition: **Fern Riverwood** (Woodsman, Lvl 1). Jonathan Don-Geon was searching for someone able to decipher the glyphic text he had copied last week, though unfortunately there were no resident scholars in the village. He did however manage to find a self-proclaimed "armchair historian" named **Ozymandias**, clearly a well-to-do fellow in his fifties, who studied the ancient peoples as a hobby. He offered the party 600 sigloi to show him the ruins in person, on the provision they were able to bring him back alive.<br>

With another heavily humid day heralding later storms, the party made their way to the ruins. Ozymandias was quick to fill any potential awkward silence with anything at all; most of what he spoke of was manifestly uninteresting, but he mentioned that the jungle was once much smaller than it is today, and had grown substantially over the previous centuries.<br>

As the party once again entered the central structure that housed the entrance to the mighty spiral stairway, Ozymandias was quick to recognise that the flanking pillars were covered in various prayers of protection. He translated the threatening text on the north wall as: "HERE LIETH KING GIDIM / BEWARE ALL YE WHO DARE ENTER / THESE MOST ACCURSED OF HALLS."<br>

Once down the spiral stairway, the party opted to head through the stone arch directly south. They stumbled upon a door to the east with rusted hinges, and corresponding reddish-brown stains. Continuing south, the party came across an exposed pit trap lined with sharp metal spikes. Flanking this were two statues of ancient warriors. Ozymandias believed that they depicted warriors of a culture centuries younger than the ancients who initially built the tomb itself. Jonathan Don-Geon slowly and carefully worked his way around to the eastern statue, and fastened a rope around it. Once he was safely across, the rest of the party followed suit, with the aid of the rope to stabilise themselves.<br>

Continuing south, the party found a stone archway to the east. Sticking their head through, they noted a door to the north, and a corridor continuing east. They opted to continue south, finding another similar set of statues flanking the way ahead, and beyond these a door to the west. The floor appeared to continue on as normal. Immediately suspicious, Fern Riverwood threw a pebble across. Though nothing seemed to happen, there was an almost imperceptible reverberation hinting that there was something amiss. The party decided to brave crossing the floor, drawing straws to see who would go first. Jonathan Don-Geon and Balthazar S. Hammer were able to cross with no issue, but as Fern Riverwood stepped forward, the floor gave way and opened into a spiked pit trap. Though she managed to pull herself out with the help of her compatriots, her legs had been savagely torn through, and arterial blood spurted from her femoral arteries. Attempting to tie off or cauterise the wound whilst Ozymandias watched on in horror, Fern Riverwood quickly passed out and bled to death. Ozymandias was eventually able to cross the pit with the aid of rope, though was clearly having second thoughts about the expedition. Not wanting to head back without the protection of the party, or without light, he collected himself and pressed on.<br>

Torchlight to the north signalled the arrival of **Lily Riversong** (Lake Priestess, Lvl 1). She cautiously made her way across the pit traps to join with the party to the south whilst they caught their breath. The western door was difficult to open, due to a strange subterranean grass which grew to waist-height. Lily Riversong and Ozymandias opted to stay outside of the room, which reeked of stale straw, whilst Balthazar S. Hammer and Jonathan Don-Geon began to hack at the grass towards a door spotted to the south. Noticing in their peripheral vision that the grass seemed to shift, they opted to immediately wade across, but were set upon by eight giant ticks waiting in ambush. Surprised, they frantically managed to avoid most of the ticks as they jumped, though one stuck on each of their backs, quickly followed by another on their chest. Jonathan Don-Geon slashed the tick on Balthazar S. Hammer's back with his knife, and Balthazar S. Hammer cleaved the tick on Jonathan Don-Geon's back with his battleaxe. Dodging further ticks, Jonathan Don-Geon was able to stab the giant tick on his chest. Another tick fastened itself to Balthazar S. Hammer, but Lily Riversong stepped in to stab it to death. At last the remaining ticks fled out the eastern door, past a terrorised Ozymandias.<br>

Moving through the southern door, the party heard a door slamming to the northeast. They opted to head west through a winding corridor, with the aim of rendezvousing with the apemen they had met from last week. They came across an open section with a door to the east - behind which violent buzzing could be heard - and a silent door to the west. As they continued further west, the party found a room with the smell of cooked meat, an extinguished firepit, and four apemen sleeping on animal skins. These apemen were initially aggressive until one recognised Balthazar S. Hammer and Jonathan Don-Geon. The party explained that there was a threat coming from the east, and the group prepared to face whatever was coming. Jonathan Don-Geon prepared a tripwire using rope and tent pegs, and the door was held open. Into the torchlight stepped a group of seven leechmen - hideous, smooth, grey humanoids, with concentric teeth - who took one look at the odds they were up against, and turned back.<br>

The party headed out the north door, and headed due east. They came across a corridor heading south, and yet another set of two statues. The statue to the west had a strangely wet-looking head, and held a golden sceptre worth 750sg. As Balthazar S. Hammer struck at the statue's legs in an attempt to discern if there was anything unusual, the wetness detached itself and wrapped round the party's torch, instantly plunging them into darkness. When Lily Riversong hastily lit a new torch, more wet-looking shapes were seen floating through the air towards them from the north. Balthazar S. Hammer grabbed the sceptre, and the party ran round the corner to the room with the spiral stairway, notably covered in the apemen's cave paintings. No sooner had they closed the door, and the strange shapes thudded against the wood behind them.<br>

The party headed up the southwestern stairs and back towards the village. The heavens opened and they were soaked through; the refreshing rain and sweet taste of fresh air a welcome relief from the stifling tomb. The party would live to see another day - and this time they had found treasure.<br>

**Treasure/XP Recovered:** 1350sg (450sg each).
