title: Venturing South
permalink: /2026-11-02/
---

With Sir Reginald Faetoucher and Julius Pines predisposed for the week, the party consisted of **Jonathan Don-Geon** (Hermit, Lvl 1), **Balthazar S. Hammer** (Miner, Lvl 1), and a new addition: **Fern Riverwood** (Woodsman, Lvl 1). Jonathan Don-Geon was searching for someone able to decipher the glyphic text he had copied last week, though there were no resident scholars in the village. He did however manage to find a self-proclaimed "armchair historian" named **Ozymandias**, clearly a well-to-do fellow in his fifties who studied the ancient peoples as a hobby. He offered the party 600 sigloi to shoow him the ruins in person, on the provision they were able to bring him back alive.<br>

With another heavily humid day heralding later storms, the party made their way to the ruins. Ozymandias was quick to fill any potential awkward silence with anything at all; most of what he spoke of was manifestly uninteresting, but he mentioned that the jungle was once much smaller than it is today, and had grown substantially over the previous centuries.<br>

As the party once again entered the central structure that housed the entrance to the mighty spiral stairway, Ozymandias was quick to recognise that the pillars were covered in various prayers of protection. He translated the threatening text on the north wall as: "HERE LIETH KING GIDIM / BEWARE ALL YE WHO DARE ENTER / THESE MOST ACCURSED OF HALLS."<br>

Once down the stairs, the party opted to head through the stone arch directly south. They stumbled upon a door to the east with rusted hinges, and corresponding reddish-brown stains. Continuing south, the party came across an exposed pit trap lined with sharp metal spikes. Flanking this were two statues of ancient warriors. Ozymandias believed that they depicted warriors of a culture centuries younger than the ancient Ummum who initially built the tomb itself. Jonathan Don-Geon slowly and carefully worked his way around to the western statue, and fastened a rope around it. Once he was safely across, the rest of the party followed suit, with the aid of the rope to stabilise themselves.<br>

Continuing south, the party found a stone archway to the east. Sticking their head through, they noted a door to the north, and a corridor continuing east. They opted to continue south, finding another set of statues flanking the way ahead, and beyond these a door to the west. The floor however appeared to continue on as normal. Immediately suspicious, Fern Riverwood threw a pebble across. Though nothing happened, there was an almost imperceptible reverberation hinting that there was something amiss. The party decided to brave crossing the floor, drawing straws to see who would go first. Jonathan Don-Geon and Balthazar S. Hammer were able to cross with no issue, but as Fern Riverwood stepped forward, the floor gave way and opened into a spiked pit trap. Though she managed to pull herself out with the help of her compatriots, her legs had been savagely pierced through, and arterial blood spurted from her femoral arteries. Attempting to tie off or cauterise the wound whilst Ozymandias watched on in horror, Fern Riverwood quickly passed out and bled to death. Ozymandias was eventually able to cross the pit with the aid of rope, though was clearly having second thoughts about the expedition.<br>

Torch light to the north signalled the arrival of **Lily Riversong** (Lake Priestess, Lvl 1). She cautiously made her way across the pit traps to join with the party to the south, whilst they collected themselves and caught their breath. The western door was difficult to open, due to the growth of a strange subterranean grass that grew to waist-height. Lily Riversong and Ozymandias opted to stay outside the room which reeked of stale straw, whilst Balthazar S. Hammer and Jonathan Don-Geon began to hack at the grass towards a door spotted to the south. Noticing in their peripheral vision that the grass seeemed to shift, they opted to immediately wade across, but were set upon by eight giant ticks waiting in ambush. Surprised, they frantically managed to avoid most of the ticks as they jumped on them, though one stuck on each of their backs, quickly followed by another on their chest. Jonathan Don-Geon slashed the giant tick on Balthazar S. Hammer's back with his knife, and Balthazar S. Hammer cleaved the giant tick on Jonathan Don-Geon's back with his battleaxe. Dodging further ticks, Jonathan Don-Geon was able to stab the giant tick on his chest. Another giant tick had fastened itself to Balthazar S. Hammer, but Lily Riversong stepped in to stab it to death. At last the remaining ticks fled out the eastern door, pass a terrorised Ozymandias.<br>

Moving through the southern door, the party heard a door slamming to the north east. They opted to head west through a winding corridor, with the aim of rendezvousing with the apemen they had met from last week. They came across an open section with a door to the east - behind which violent buzzing could be heard - and a silent door to the west. As they continued further west, the party found a room to the southwest.
