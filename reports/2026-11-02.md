He did however manage to find a self-proclaimed "armchair historian" named **Ozymandias**, clearly a well-to-do fellow in his fifties who studied the ancient peoples as a hobby. He offered the party 600 sigloi to shoow him the ruins in person, on the provision they were able to bring him back alive.<br>
