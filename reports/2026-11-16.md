---
title: The Mantis and the Slab
permalink: /2026-11-16/
---

**Sir Reginald Faetoucher** (Minor Noble, Lvl 1), **Balthazar S. Hammer** (Miner, Lvl 1), **Jonathan Don-Geon** (Hermit, Lvl 1), and **Julius Pines** (Gourmand, Lvl 1) decided to purchase a Giant Spider whom they called **Peter Parker**. As the party prepared to set off, they were approached by a gruff and well-built man who embedded his axe into their table at the inn. He introduced himself as **Boris Strugatsky**, and wanted in on the expedition after having heard of the previously recovered treasure. The party agreed to take him along at the price of a half-share of what they managed to recover.<br>

The party approached the ruins during yet another oppressively humid day, complete with accompanying sound of distant rolling thunder. They opted to take the southwestern staircase; the path they had cut previously was becoming noticeably overgrown. Sir Reginald Faetoucher lit his lantern, and the party descended the spiral staircase marked by distinctive cave paintings. They opted to head east. Growing out of the floor of the corridor were strange flowers in various secondary colours: green, orange, purple. Stumbling upon a door to the north, the party entered a large room with a faded mural painted in ochre and lapis lazuli. The mural seemed to depict the gathering of saintly warriors and various sacred items, along with the mustering of an army. Amongst these murals were words written in neat glyphic text, which were copied by Jonathan Don-Geon for translation later. Julius Pines picked a handful of flowers.<br>

The party headed out of the room to explore further east, then north. They found a door to the east, and to the north a stone arch, through which were the south-facing sides of two pillars. Entering the eastern room, they stirred up dust, and discovered further murals depicting what appeared to be an epic battle between good and evil, featuring some of the saintly heroes from the previous room defeating an amorphous mass of monstrous foes. Once again they found neat glyphic text, which was again transcribed by Julius Pines.<br>

Heading north, the party found further pillars, all painted with ochre and lapis, and stone archways to the north, east, and west. Entering into their torchlight from the murky darkness to the west was a fearsome giant mantis. After a succession of attempted blows - including a narrowly missed javelin - the mantis managed to inflict a flesh wound on Boris Strugatsky, before Balthazar S. Hammer moved in with his battleaxe, and in one mighty blow decapitated the foul insectoid. Julius Pines attempted to cook the mantis whilst the party caught their breath, but the resulting meal was absolutely vile.<br>

As the party rested, they noticed a loose section of the floor featuring an ornately painted mural valued at 1750sg. Using a crowbar and the strength of multiple men, they managed to loosen the slab and fix it to Peter Parker, just as a hunting party of six lizardmen charged in from the south. After a volley of bone javelins - one hitting the pillars, one hitting Julius Pine's cooking pot - the two groups exchanged a series of attacks. Two of the lizardmen were slain by Balthazar S. Hammer's  battle-axe, and one was brained by Jonathan Don-Geon's staff. Julius Pines slew a lizardmen with his mace as the lizardman simultaneously pierced him with a potentially mortal wound, and Sir Reginald Faetoucher was also taken out of the fight by a piercing blow. The remaining two lizardmen fled, leaving the party to frantically attempt to stabilise Julius Pines and Sir Reginald Faetoucher. Without bandages this took longer than they wanted, but fortunately their comrades had sufficient remaining strength to hold out.<br>

With Julius Pines and Sir Reginald Faetoucher back on their feet but weak, the party sought to leave the dungeon. They headed west, and ran into doors heading north and south. Behind the northern door was the stench of death, a dismembered apeman corpse, and bloody handprints on the walls. The corpse was covered in fist-sized moths. Julius Pines threw in a lit rag causing the moths to startle, before promptly closing the door.<br>

Heading back east to the room with the pillars and then south, the party encountered strange translucent shapes floating through the air. Lighting torches using Reginald Faetoucher's lantern, they managed to distract the shapes; attracted to heat, they wrapped themselves around the flames extinguishing both the light and their existence.<br>

The party headed for the southwestern stairs and left the complex; the flowers Julius Pines had picked immediately wilted upon exiting. Grateful that they had Peter Parker to carry their heavy treasure back to the village, the party returned under lightning-filled stormy skies.<br>

**Treasure/XP Recovered:** 1750sg (389sg each).<br>
