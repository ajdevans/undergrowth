**Sir Reginald Faetoucher** (Minor Noble, Lvl 1),** Balthazar S. Hammer** (Miner, Lvl 1), **Jonathan Don-Geon** (Hermit, Lvl 1), and **Julius Pines** (Gourmand, Lvl 1) decided to buy a Giant Spider whom they called **Peter Parker**. As the party prepared to set off, they were approached by a gruff and well-built man who embedded his axe into the table. He introduced himself as **Boris Strugatsky**, who after hearing of the treasure recovered previously wanted in on the expedition.<br>

The party approached the ruins during yet another oppressively humid day, complete with accompanying sound of distant rolling thunder. They opted to take the southwestern staircase; the path they had cut was becoming noticeably overgrown. 

**Treasure/XP Recovered:** 1750sg (389sg each).<br>
