Under thunderous and angry skies, our intrepid party ventured forth: **Sir Reginald Fae Toucher** (Minor Noble, Lvl 1), **Jeremy Pines** (Chinampas Farmer, Lvl 1), **Balthasar S. Hammer** (Miner, Lvl 1), and **John don Geon** (Dock Worker, Lvl 1). Approaching the reportedly cursed and haunted ruins of the Tomb of Gidim, they entered a structure coated with greenery. A mighty spiral staircase lay before them, flanked by pillars. Upon the north wall beyond the staircase was a message written in glyphic -- the writing system of the ancients -- and though indecipherable, the manner in which it was written seemed ominously threatening.<br>

Descending down the stairway, the party came across a room with four exits: north, east, south, and west. Heading north, they stumbled upon corridors carpeted in moss, and doors swollen in their frames and stiff to open.
