## Principles:
I. Ask questions always. Leverage the situation.<br>
II. Think laterally. Scheme to avoid rolling.<br>
III. Equipment is your class. Prepare wisely.<br>
IV. Combat is dangerous. Fight unfairly. Know when to run.<br>
V. Play to win. Delight in losing.<br>

---

## ROLLING DICE
**Skill Rolls:** When trying to execute a plan where success is not guaranteed, roll 1d6. The X-in-6 chance is dependent on preparation and planning.<br>

**Saves:** Avoid danger in risky situations. Roll 1d20 under or equal to ability score. 1 is always a success, 20 is always a failure.<br>

---

## THE DUNGEON
**Turns:** Time is broken down into Turns lasting 10-minutes. Examples of Turns include entering a new room, moving through three explored rooms, or excessive time spent deliberating.<br>

**Lighting:** Torches last six Turns or 60 minutes each, lanterns require oil and burn for 24 Turns or 4 hours. Both illuminate a 30ft radius.<br>

**Wandering Monsters:** Every 2 Turns there is a 1-in-6 chance of encountering a monster.<br>

**Reactions:** When encountering another being, 2d6 is rolled to determine their temperament. Higher is friendlier, or less aggressive. Not every encounter needs to lead to bloodshed.<br>

**Movement:** By default, you are moving slowly and stealthily. You always notice hazards and traps unless running, visually impaired, or distracted.<br>

**Traps:** If mechanical, not guaranteed to activate. Some traps may be deadly, resulting in instant death on a failed Saving Throw.<br>

**Searching:** You can spend a Turn for a 1-in-6 chance of finding everything hidden in a 10x10ft space, including secret passageways.<br>

**Doors:** DX save to lockpick; takes 1 Turn. ST save to force open; takes 1 Turn, noisy. WP save to listen at a door; takes 1 Turn. Iron spikes can be used to wedge doors shut.<br>

**Leaving the Dungeon:** You must leave the dungeon before the session ends. You need to know a safe route, have enough lighting, and avoid wandering monsters.<br>

## CONDITIONS
**Fatigued:** +2 to DX Saves until you rest for 1 Turn.<br>
**Poisoned:** +2 to ST Saves for 1d4 days.<br>
**Diseased:** +2 to ST Saves for 1d4 weeks.<br>
**Deranged:** +2 to WP Saves for 1d4 months.<br>
**Cursed:** +2 to WP Saves until removed by priest.<br>

---

## DAMAGE AND DEATH

**Damage:** Armour reduces incoming damage. Damage reduces HP. After HP is gone, you are wounded, and damage reduces ST. If this happens, make a ST save with to avoid taking Critical Damage.<br>

**Critical Damage:** You are down and dying. You cannot take further action until Stabilised. Every Turn out of combat your ST reduces by 1. If your ST score reaches 0, you are dead.<br>

**Stabilising:** Attempts take a Turn. There is a 1-in-6 chance of Stabilising without bandages, and a 3-in-6 chance with bandages.<br>

**Paralysis and Catatonia:** If DX reaches 0, you are paralysed, taking 1d6 months to recover. If WP reaches 0, you are catatonic, taking 1d12+1 months to recover.<br>

---

## RESTING
**Short Rest:** Resting for a Turn and drinking recovers all lost HP. You will need to rest every Six Turns or suffer Fatigue.<br>

**Full Rest:** Resting for a week restores 1d6 of each Ability Score, at the cost of 0.5% of your XP Total 	in sigloi to maintain equipment and cover lifestyle expenses.<br>

**Special Rest:** Resting for a week at the cost of 1% of your XP Total in sigloi can remove either the Diseased or Deranged condition in addition to restoring 1d6 of each Ability Score.<br>
