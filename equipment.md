**Starting Equipment:** Every character starts with a Backpack, Clothes fitting their profession, Knife (d6 blade damage), Torches (6), Tinderbox, Waterskin (3 Uses).<br>
**Selling:** Equipment can be sold for half the buying price.<br>
**Encumbrance:** You have Equipment Slots equal to your max ST. Most items take up one slot. 500 sigloi takes up a slot.<br>
**Bulky:** Items with the Bulky tag take up two equipment slots and require two hands to use.<br>
**Slow:** Weapons with the Slow tag act last, as if they had lost initiative.<br>
**Reach:** Can hit targets at a greater distance in melee.<br>

**Blunt:** +1 vs armour.<br>
**Blade:** +1 vs unarmoured, -1 vs armoured.<br>
**Pierce:** No effect.<br>
**Axe:** +1 vs shield.<br>

# VENDORS

## THE ARMOURER
**Light Armour (100sg):** Armour 1. Padded gambeson with helmet.
**Full Armour (1000sg):** Armour 2. Lamellar armour with helmet, gauntlet, greaves. Makes it very hard to run, move quietly, swim, leap etc. Bulky.
**Shield (20sg):** Armour 1. Can shatter once to absorb all damage. Bulky.
